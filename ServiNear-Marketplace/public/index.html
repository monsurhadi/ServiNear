QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked

When is contact information shared?

The customer’s phone and exact address are hidden from public task listings and appear to the selected provider only after the customer accepts their offer.

What does verified mean?

It means an admin has marked the provider profile as verified in this demo. Real identity checks must be completed by the platform before a public launch.

How do I report a concern?

Use the support form and include the task title or ID. Requests are stored for admin review; this demo does not provide emergency dispatch.

Is payment handled in the app?

No. Agree on payment directly and use your own judgment. Online payments and escrow are not connected.
