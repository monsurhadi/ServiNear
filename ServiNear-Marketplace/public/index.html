    QUICK ANSWERS

    Frequently asked

    When is contact information shared?

    The customer’s phone and exact address are hidden from public task listings and appear to the selected provider only after the customer accepts their offer.

    What does NID verified mean?

    It means our review team has inspected the provider's National ID (front & back) and verified identity with a live selfie.

    How do I report a concern?

    Use the support form and include the task title or ID. Requests are stored for immediate admin team review.

    Is payment handled in the app?

    No. Customers pay providers directly via bKash, Nagad, or Cash on completion with zero commission deductions.
